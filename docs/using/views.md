# Views

This page is for anyone using Invy. It shows how to keep a search you use often as a view, choose how it is shown,
and pin it to **Find** so it is the first thing you see.

A view is a [search](search.md) with a name and a way of showing what it finds. It runs again each time you open
it, so a view of `seen>1y` always means "not seen in the year before today", and a thing drops out of it as soon as
someone scans it.

## Make a view

1. Search on **Find**, then press **Save as a view** above the results. Or open the **Views** tab and press
   **New view**.
2. Give it a **Name**, such as "Not seen in a year".
3. Check the **Search**. It is written as in the Find box; leave it empty to find everything.
4. Choose how to **Show as**:

   | Display | Shows |
   |---|---|
   | **Table** | Rows with the **Columns** you tick. Press a column's heading to sort by it. |
   | **Cards** | A card per item, with its first photo. |
   | **Tree** | The items under the containers they are in. |
   | **Board** | A column per choice of a field you **Group by**, such as condition or power. It needs a choice field on a type. |
   | **Timeline** | What happened to the items it finds, newest first. |

5. Choose an **Order** if you want one other than best match.
6. Switch on **Pin to Find** to show it on **Find** under the search box, with its count and first few items.
7. Press **Make view**.

## Share a view

Views are yours alone unless you switch on **Share with everyone in this space**. Editors and owners can share
views and change shared ones. A viewer can open a shared view and press **Save a copy** to make their own.

A pinned shared view shows on everyone's **Find**.

## Change or remove a view

Open it on **Views**, then press **Pin to Find** or **Unpin**, **Change**, or **Remove**. Removing a view never
changes the items it found. **Export CSV** downloads everything it finds as a spreadsheet; see
[Reports](reports.md#download-a-spreadsheet).

## If something goes wrong

- **A view says a field or type doesn't exist.** Its search names something that was renamed or removed. Press
  **Change the search** and fix it; the message says which part no longer reads.
- **A view is gone.** Someone removed it, or it was someone else's and wasn't shared. Pick another view.
- **A count starts with "About".** Above 10,000 items Invy shows an estimate, as on [Find](search.md).
