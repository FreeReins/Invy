# Moving house

This page is for anyone packing for a move. It shows how to say where each box goes in the new place, print a
manifest to stick on it, and unpack each box into its room with one tap.

Moving shows while the space has the **Moving** feature on (**Settings → Kind and features**). See
[Spaces](spaces.md#features).

## Before you pack

Add the new place's rooms, for example a *New house* at the top with *Kitchen* and *Bedroom* inside it, so boxes
have somewhere to go.

## Say where a box goes

1. Open the box on **Browse**.
2. Press **Say where it's going**, browse to the room, and press **Going to …**.

In search, `going:kitchen` finds the boxes going to the kitchen. **Views → Moving** lists every box with a
destination.

## Print a manifest

Press **Manifest** on the box's page, or next to it in **Moving**, then **Print or save as PDF**. It shows the
box's name and label code, where it goes in large type, and what's inside it.

## When a box arrives

Press **Arrived** on the box's page, or in **Moving**. The box moves into its destination with everything inside
it, as one move in the timeline, and the destination is cleared.

## If something goes wrong

- **"… has no destination".** Say where it's going first, or use **Move**.
- **"… can't go inside itself".** A box can't be going into something that's inside it.
