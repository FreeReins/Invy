# Lending

This page is for anyone who lends things out: a drill to a neighbour, a projector to the church hall, a laptop to a
colleague. It shows how to mark something lent, see what's out, and get reminded when it's due back.

Lending shows while the space has the **Lending** feature on (**Settings → Kind and features**). See
[Spaces](spaces.md#features).

## Lend something

1. Open the thing on **Browse**.
2. Press **Lend**.
3. In **Lent to**, write who or where it went, such as *Sam* or *the church hall*.
4. Optionally pick **Due back**, the day it should come back.
5. Press **Lend**.

The whole thing is lent: its status becomes **Lent**, and its timeline gets a *lent* entry with who has it. A
counted thing is lent as one lot.

## When it comes back

Press **It's back** on its page, or next to it in the **Lent out** list. Its status goes back to active, and the
timeline gets a *returned* entry.

## See what's out

- **Views → Lent out** lists everything lent, with who has it and when it's due, soonest first. Overdue loans are
  marked **Overdue**.
- In search, `is:lent` finds everything lent, `is:overdue` what's past its due date, and `lent:sam` what's with
  someone whose name contains *sam*.

## Reminders

- The daily reminders include loans due back today or overdue (type `overdue`).
- A loan's due date is in the space's calendar feed; see [Reminders](reminders.md#the-calendar-feed).

## If something goes wrong

- **"… is already lent to …"** Mark it returned first, then lend it again.
- **"… can't be lent".** It's missing, disposed of or archived. Restore or find it first.
- **"A loan is due back between today and ten years from now".** Pick another date, or leave it empty.
