# Getting help with Invy

1. **Read the docs.** [Troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting/index.md) lists common problems and their fixes. The
   same pages are on the [documentation site](https://freereins.github.io/invy/) and inside Invy under **?**.
2. **Search the issues.** Someone may have had the same problem:
   [open and closed issues](https://github.com/FreeReins/invy/issues?q=is%3Aissue).
3. **Open an issue.** [Choose a template](https://github.com/FreeReins/invy/issues/new/choose),
   and include your version, how you installed Invy, and the log.

Security problems are reported privately instead: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
